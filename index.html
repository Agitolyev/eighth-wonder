    5 · Limitations & risks

    This is a simplified projection, not a forecast. It compounds a single return you pick, at a steady rate, in a straight line — the real world is neither steady nor straight. Read these before trusting any number above.

    What the model leaves out

    • Constant return. Every period earns the exact rate you typed. Real payouts swing year to year — occupancy, rent reviews, refinancing and outright down years are all ignored.
    • No fees, taxes or inflation. Management fees, entry and exit costs, income and capital-gains tax, and inflation eroding real purchasing power are not deducted.
    • Static exchange rate. Cross-currency figures use one saved FX snapshot, not live or future rates.
    • Perfect execution. It assumes payouts always arrive on time and reinvest instantly, with certificates always available to buy.

    Real-world risks it can’t price

    • Fund default & sponsor risk. The fund, its manager or the underlying property owner can underperform, suspend payouts, restructure or fail outright — you can lose some or all of your capital.
    • Currency devaluation. The hryvnia (or any payout currency) can lose value sharply, cutting the real worth of both payouts and capital measured in dollars or euros.
    • Political & geopolitical risk. War, martial law, capital controls, payment moratoria, expropriation, sanctions and abrupt regulatory or tax changes can freeze payouts, block conversions or impair the assets.
    • Liquidity risk. These certificates are not freely traded — you may be unable to sell when you want, or only at a steep discount.
    • Market & property risk. Falling valuations, tenant defaults, damage and rising vacancy can reduce or wipe out returns.

    Partly for reasons like these, many investors choose not to rely on a single fund. Spreading money across several funds, different sectors and other kinds of assets is a common way to soften the impact if any one of them disappoints. This tool models one option at a time — the comparison above is there to help you weigh a few side by side.
